---
name: Planned budget separation
description: Procurement budget and product pricing are deliberately independent values.
---

Planned budget must be entered and maintained independently of product unit price and quantity.

**Why:** The planned budget is the amount committed to the project; unit price and quantity describe a product and must not silently alter portfolio or project budgets.

**How to apply:** Use the stored planned-budget value for all budget totals, remaining-budget calculations, and reports. Preserve product price and quantity as descriptive procurement details only.
